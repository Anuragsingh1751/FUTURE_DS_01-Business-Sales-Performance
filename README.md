## 📊 Project Overview

Portfolio dashboard for **Future Interns — Data Science & Analytics Task 1: Business Sales Performance Analytics**.

The project analyzes sales, profit, orders, monthly trends, product categories, regional performance, and top products, then converts the findings into actionable business recommendations.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

1. **Technology** is the strongest revenue category; protect product availability and cross-sell complementary products.
2. **West** leads regional sales and can be used as a benchmark while building a targeted improvement plan for South.
3. **Laptops** are the leading product by sales; prioritize stock coverage and bundled offers.
4. Review high-discount transactions and loss-making products before scaling promotions.
5. Track sales and profit together each month so revenue growth does not hide margin pressure.
