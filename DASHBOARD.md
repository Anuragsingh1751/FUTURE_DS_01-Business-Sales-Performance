# Dashboard Overview

## Business Sales Performance Analytics

The completed dashboard is an Excel-based portfolio dashboard covering the 2024–2025 analysis period.

### KPI Snapshot

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $1,851,244 |
| Total Profit | $234,658 |
| Total Orders | 5,000 |
| Average Order Value | $370 |
| Profit Margin | 12.7% |

### Visualizations

1. **Monthly Sales Trend** — tracks month-by-month revenue movement.
2. **Sales by Category** — compares Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.
3. **Regional Sales & Profit** — compares West, East, Central, and South.
4. **Top 10 Products by Sales** — ranks products by revenue contribution.

### Executive Takeaway

Technology is the leading category, West leads regional sales, and Laptops are the top product by revenue. The analysis recommends protecting availability, using regional benchmarks, prioritizing Laptop stock, reviewing high-discount/loss-making transactions, and tracking profit alongside sales.
