# Project Summary

## Business Problem
Businesses need a clear view of revenue, profit, orders, regional performance, product performance, and category contribution to make better inventory, pricing, promotion, and sales decisions.

## Analysis Performed
The dashboard summarizes monthly sales, category sales, regional sales and profit, and the top 10 products by sales. KPI cards provide a high-level performance snapshot.

## Recommendations
- Protect availability of high-performing Technology products.
- Use West-region performance as a benchmark for other regions.
- Prioritize Laptop stock and explore bundles/cross-sell opportunities.
- Investigate high-discount and loss-making transactions.
- Track sales and profit together to protect margins during growth.

## Deliverable
The main deliverable is the Excel-based portfolio dashboard included in the repository.
